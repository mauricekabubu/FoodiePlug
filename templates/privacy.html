1. About this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how FoodiePlug collects, uses, shares, and protects information in connection with our meal-planning, grocery, and food-decision services (the "Services"). It applies whenever you create an account, use the app or website, or otherwise interact with FoodiePlug.

We've tried to write this in plain language rather than dense legal jargon. If anything is unclear, or you have a question about your information, you can reach us using the contact details in Section 18.

2. Information We Collect

We collect a few categories of information to run FoodiePlug: information you give us directly, information generated by your use of food-planning features, and basic technical information collected automatically.

Account information

  • Full name
  • Username
  • Email address
  • Password / authentication credentials
  • Account preferences

Food information you provide

  • Meal preferences
  • Grocery items
  • Food choices
  • Ingredients
  • Budget information
  • User-entered prices

Technical information

  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Operating system
  • IP address
  • Log & usage information

This list reflects the categories FoodiePlug actually collects to provide its features — it will be finalized against the live implementation before publication.

3. Information You Provide

You may provide information directly when you:

  • Create an account
  • Update your profile
  • Create meal plans
  • Enter groceries or ingredients
  • Enter food prices or budgets
  • Interact with FoodiePlug features
  • Contact support or submit feedback

4. Information Collected Automatically

Some information is collected automatically as part of operating the Services:

  • Device and browser information
  • IP address
  • Pages and features accessed
  • Approximate usage information
  • Diagnostic and log information

Necessary technical information (like login sessions) is distinct from optional analytics, which is only collected where actually implemented — we do not imply tracking technologies that aren't in use.

5. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Authenticate you when you log in
  • Provide meal-planning functionality
  • Personalize food recommendations
  • Manage grocery planning and budget estimates
  • Improve FoodiePlug over time
  • Respond to support requests
  • Maintain platform security and detect abuse
  • Communicate important service information
  • Analyze product usage where applicable

6. Meal Planning & Personalization

Preferences you share — like dietary needs, favorite ingredients, or budget targets — help us tailor meal suggestions, grocery recommendations, food ideas, and meal schedules to you. Personalization exists to make FoodiePlug more useful, not to build a profile beyond what's needed for that purpose.

7. AI-Powered Features

Some FoodiePlug features use AI to generate recommendations, suggestions, or responses — for example, meal ideas based on what's in your fridge.

About AI-powered features

Information you provide to an AI-powered feature may be processed in order to generate that feature's output. AI-generated results may not always be accurate, so use judgment before relying on them. Avoid submitting information you don't want processed by a given feature. The specific AI provider(s) and their data-handling practices will be identified here once finalized.

8. Grocery & Price Information

You may voluntarily enter grocery prices, ingredient costs, shopping lists, and food-budget information. We use this to power budget calculations, grocery planning, meal recommendations, and personalized food decisions.

User-entered prices reflect what you or other users have logged, and may not represent current market prices.

10. How We Share Information

We share information with service providers and third parties only where necessary to operate the Services, meet legal requirements, or for other purposes described here — never broadly or without reason. Categories may include:

  • Hosting and infrastructure providers
  • Authentication providers
  • Email providers
  • AI service providers
  • Analytics providers
  • Payment providers, if applicable

Specific providers actually used by FoodiePlug will be named here rather than described generically.

11. Third-Party Services

FoodiePlug may integrate with external services such as Google authentication, email delivery, AI providers, cloud hosting, or payment processors. Each provider actually used will be listed here along with what's shared and why.

Third-party services have their own privacy policies and terms, which we encourage you to review.
