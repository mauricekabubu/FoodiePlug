How these numbers are calculated
  • Spending adds up expenses you recorded in your budgets. Planned or listed items are never counted as spending.
  • Comparisons use the previous period of the same length, and only appear when that period has recorded expenses.
  • Estimates (such as the pace estimate) are labelled and are calculations, not forecasts.
  • Pantry checks compare quantities in the same unit. If units differ, an ingredient counts as available when you hold any of that food.
  • Not available yet: spending by category, meal and shopping costs, price changes, and food-waste totals. These need categories on expenses, prices, purchase dates and recorded disposals.
