About FoodiePlug AI

FoodiePlug may use AI-powered features to help generate meal ideas, planning suggestions, and food-related recommendations.

  • AI responses may occasionally be inaccurate.
  • Recommendations should be reviewed before use.
  • Food prices and availability can change.
  • AI recommendations are not a substitute for professional medical or nutritional advice.

Account & Login

How do I reset my password?

If you've forgotten your password, you can set a new one in a few quick steps.

  1. Open the FoodiePlug login page.
  2. Select "Forgot password?"
  3. Enter your email address.
  4. Follow the password-reset instructions we send you.
  5. Create your new password.
Note: the reset link expires after a short time for security. If it's expired, just request a new one.
