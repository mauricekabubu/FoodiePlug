COMMON QUESTIONS

Getting started with FoodiePlug.

What is FoodiePlug?

FoodiePlug is a food-planning platform designed to help people organise meals, manage pantry items, plan shopping and make more informed food-budget decisions.

Can FoodiePlug help me plan affordable meals in Kenya?

FoodiePlug is designed with everyday Kenyan food habits in mind. You can use its planning tools to organise meals and groceries around your needs. Actual food costs vary by location, season and shop.

Can I organise my groceries and pantry?

Yes. FoodiePlug includes pantry and shopping functionality to help you organise ingredients and keep track of what you need to buy.

Does FoodiePlug include an AI assistant?

FoodiePlug includes an AI assistant intended to help with food-related questions and planning. Its responses should be treated as guidance, not a guarantee of prices or nutritional outcomes.

How do I get started?

Create an account to get started with FoodiePlug and explore the tools available to you.
